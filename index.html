Kraken Rising Privacy Policy

App: Kraken Rising: Octopus Attack (Android, package com.vulturetech.krakenrising)
Developer: Vulture Tech
Effective date: 17 November 2026

Summary

Kraken Rising is an action game that works fully offline. It has no ads, no in-app purchases and no accounts. If, and only if, you choose Allow when the game asks to share usage stats (or turn on Settings → Share usage stats), the app sends gameplay statistics and crash reports to us through Google Firebase. If you choose No thanks, or never answer, nothing is sent. Stats from before you answer stay on your device and are sent only if you allow. You can change your choice at any time.

Information stored on your device

To save your game, the app stores these on your device only: your pearls, the octopuses you have unlocked and their upgrades, Shop upgrades, mission progress, places you have found, relics, best scores, your settings (sound, motion, quality, button layout), the date the rating dialog was last requested, your usage-stats choice, and whether you have seen the tutorial and opening scene. Apart from the optional usage stats described below, this information never leaves your device through the app. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app. If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this game data in your own device backup; that backup belongs to you and is not accessible to us.

Optional usage stats and crash reports (Google Firebase)

Only if you opt in, the app uses two Google services: Firebase Analytics (gameplay statistics) and Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports). Google processes this data for us as our service provider under the Firebase data processing terms and Google's Privacy Policy.

What is sentWhy
Gameplay events: run started and ended (with the octopus, game mode, score, run length, highest wanted level and cause of the run's end), first-time steps (such as the opening scene, the first move, the first meal and the first run's end), tutorial steps, runs left part-way (when the app is closed or put in the background during a run), the game's frame rate and graphics quality, missions completed, octopuses and upgrades unlocked (with their pearl price), areas and hidden places found, treasure opened, wanted level changes, and a few special moments (for example a Deep Frenzy). Also standard app-usage information that Firebase records automatically, such as first open, session start and length, app version, and when the app was updated.To see which parts of the game are too hard or too easy, and improve the game
Crash reports: technical details when the app crashes or hits an error (the error message and code location, the time, the app version and state, whether the app was in the foreground, free memory and disk space).To find and fix bugs
Device information: device model and manufacturer, Android version, language, screen size and pixel density, approximate memory size and number of processor cores, and the country or region (approximate location that Google works out from the IP address; the IP address itself is not stored by Analytics and is not shown to us).To understand errors and which devices to test on
Identifiers: random identifiers created by Firebase for this installation of the app (the Analytics app-instance ID, the Firebase installation ID and a Crashlytics installation ID). They are not linked to your name, email, phone number or Google account.To count players and group events and crashes from the same install

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, precise location, photos, files or anything you type. The app does not use the advertising ID or the Android ID, does not collect data for advertising, does not use Google Signals, and does not share or sell data to anyone. Analytics advertising features (ad storage, ad user data and ad personalization) are always switched off. All data is sent encrypted (HTTPS).

How to turn it off

Go to Settings → Share usage stats and switch it off. The app then stops sending data straight away, asks Firebase to reset the Analytics identifier for your install, and deletes any crash reports that have not yet been sent.

Ratings and reviews through Google Play

After a happy moment in the game (unlocking a new octopus), the app may ask Google Play to show its standard rating dialog, at most once every 30 days. Whether the dialog appears is decided by Google Play. If you choose to submit a star rating or a written review there, it is handled by Google Play and published on the Play Store under Google's Privacy Policy, just as when you rate an app in the Play Store. If the app is installed from a test track, Google Play shares that feedback privately with us instead of publishing it. The app itself never sees your rating or review, and you never have to give one: no reward depends on it.

Permissions

PermissionWhy
Internet, network stateSending optional usage stats and crash reports, only if you opt in (network state lets Firebase check for a connection before sending).

Internet access is used only to send the optional usage stats and crash reports described above, and only after you opt in. The game itself does not need the internet. The app does not use the advertising ID, precise location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Children

Kraken Rising contains cartoon action (a giant octopus grabs boats, people and police vehicles, with no blood) and is designed for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has turned on usage stats, contact us and we will delete the related data.

Data retention and deletion (how to delete your data)

Firebase Analytics event data is kept for 2 months, then deleted automatically; summary reports (counts, without identifiers) may be kept longer. Crashlytics crash reports are kept for up to 90 days. To have the data for your install deleted, first switch off Settings → Share usage stats (this resets the Analytics identifier), then email us at the address below; because the data is not linked to your identity, it helps if you tell us roughly when you played. We will delete what we can find within 30 days. Game data on your device stays until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes what information is handled, we will update this policy and the effective date before that version is released, and describe the change in the Play Store listing's Data safety section.
